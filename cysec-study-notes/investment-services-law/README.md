## What this chapter covers

Law 87(I)/2017 transposes the EU's MiFID II Directive and has applied in Cyprus since 3 January 2018. It sets out whom the rules on investment services cover, which firms and activities are exempt, and how firms from outside the EU may serve clients in Cyprus. The first notes cover the Law's scope, third-country firms and the exemptions.

The middle notes deal with the supervisors, CySEC and the Central Bank of Cyprus: their powers, their cooperation with other authorities, and the offences the Law creates. The last group explains the Investor Compensation Fund: who must join, how it is funded and invested, and when and how much it pays.

Several points in the exam material are out of date: data reporting service providers left the Law in January 2022, the commodity-dealer exemption has needed no yearly notice since February 2022, and CySEC now sets how often it reviews each investment firm by the firm's size and complexity, instead of reviewing every firm at least once a year. Each note teaches what the exam tests and says what applies today.
