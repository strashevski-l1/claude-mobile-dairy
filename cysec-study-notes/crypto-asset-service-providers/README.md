## What this chapter covers

From 2021 Cyprus required crypto-asset service providers to register with CySEC under Article 61E of the AML/CFT Law and CySEC's Directive for the register of CASPs. The exam material tests that national regime: who had to register, the application, suspension, changes, and the organisational requirements.

The law has moved on. The EU's MiCA Regulation has applied to CASPs since 30 December 2024, CySEC stopped taking national applications in October 2024, and since 1 July 2026 crypto-asset services may be provided only under MiCA. The AML/CFT duties of CASPs continue, now with the EU travel rule for crypto-asset transfers.

Read the notes in order the first time: the register and its replacement, applications and changes, suspension, then how a CASP had to be organised and its AML/CFT obligations.
