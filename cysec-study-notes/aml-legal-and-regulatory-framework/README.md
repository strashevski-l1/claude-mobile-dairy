## What this chapter covers

Cyprus's fight against money laundering rests on Law 188(I)/2007, which sets up the financial intelligence unit MOKAS, names the supervisors that police regulated firms, and creates the criminal offences and penalties that apply to launderers and to professionals who fail in their duties.

That national law sits inside a wider framework: EU directives adopted by the European Parliament and the Council, the global standards of the Financial Action Task Force, evaluations by the Council of Europe's MONEYVAL, and separate laws on terrorism and sanctions, the latter reformed in July 2025.

Read the notes in order the first time. The first two cover the authorities in Cyprus, the next two the offences, then the international bodies, and finally the terrorism and sanctions laws.
