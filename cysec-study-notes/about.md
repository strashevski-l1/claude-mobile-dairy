# How we write study notes

ExamPass CY study notes are free explanations of the topics tested in the CySEC AML, Advanced and Basic certification exams. This page explains who writes them, which sources they rely on and how they are kept accurate.

## Who writes the notes?

Every note is written and reviewed by the ExamPass CY editorial team, a team of finance professionals who sat the CySEC exams themselves. The same team prepares the practice questions, explanations and flashcards on this site.

## Which sources do we use?

We work from primary sources: the Cyprus AML/CFT Law (Law 188(I)/2007, as amended), CySEC directives, EU regulations and directives, and official guidance such as the European Banking Authority's guidelines. Each note lists the provisions it relies on, with links where an official text is available online.

Where official sources disagree and we cannot tell which is right, we leave the disputed figure out rather than risk teaching the wrong one. Where a rule has changed recently, the note gives the current rule and, where it helps, the earlier figure and the date it changed, because some study material still shows the older version.

## How are the notes written?

Every note answers one question. It opens with a short answer, sets out the facts worth remembering in a table, explains the reasoning and lists the mistakes candidates commonly make. Everything is written in our own words; we do not reproduce course materials or publish exam questions and their answers.

Where an important change has been adopted but has not yet taken effect, such as parts of the EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation that applies from 10 July 2027, we mention it in a separate box and describe the rules in force today.

## How are the notes kept up to date?

Each note shows the date it was last reviewed against the law in force on that date. We aim to recheck notes when the underlying law changes, and we update the review date whenever we do.

## How do I report an error?

If you think something is wrong or out of date, use the Report an error link at the end of each note. We correct confirmed errors as soon as we can and update the review date.

## Is this legal advice?

No. The notes are written to help candidates prepare for the CySEC exams. They are not legal advice, and firms should rely on the official texts and their own advisers for compliance decisions. ExamPass CY is not affiliated with the Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission (CySEC).
